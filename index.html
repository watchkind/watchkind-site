Beyond specs

What makes a watch worth wearing?

01

Design

Proportion, visual hierarchy, detail, character and whether the whole thing actually feels coherent.

02

Engineering

How intelligently the movement, case, materials and technical choices solve the job the watch is built to do.

03

Originality

Real identity over borrowed status. Inspiration is normal; imitation should not be the entire product story.

04

Build

Not just the ingredients on the spec sheet — construction, finishing, tactile quality and how well they come together.

05

Wearability

Diameter is only the beginning. Geometry, span, thickness, curvature and balance decide how a watch really wears.

06

Value

Cheap is not automatically good value — and expensive is not automatically bad value. What matters is what you actually get.
