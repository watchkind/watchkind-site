Three things to know

The score is more useful when you can see its shape.

01 · Design + originality

Familiar doesn't automatically mean derivative.

The PRX's clearest strength is coherent identity with real own-lineage credibility. The modern design traces back to Tissot's 1978 language.

02 · Movement

80 hours is useful — but it isn't the whole movement.

The reserve meaningfully changes ownership behaviour. Accuracy, architecture and serviceability remain solid rather than exceptional, keeping the full movement story grounded.

03 · Wearability

40 mm is not the whole fit story.

Integrated first links create an effective wrist span of roughly 51.5 mm. That makes fit more sensitive and reduces easy strap experimentation.
